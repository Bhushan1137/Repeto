# **17\. Account Deletion and Data Rights**

Repeto provides account-deletion functionality directly within the application.

The account deletion option is available through:

**Profile → Account Actions → Delete Account**

Repeto provides two deletion pathways.

## **17.1 Deactivate Account — 30-Day Recovery Period**

When a user selects the standard account-deletion option, the account may enter a **30-day recovery period**.

During this period:

* The account is marked for deletion;  
* Access may be restricted according to the deletion state;  
* The user may log back in to recover the account; and  
* Previously associated routines and household memberships may be restored.

If the user restores the account within the applicable recovery period, the deletion state may be cleared and the account returned to active status.

## **17.2 Start Fresh — Immediate Permanent Wipe**

Repeto also provides a **Start Fresh / Permanent Wipe** option.

When this option is selected, Repeto is designed to permanently remove the applicable account information and associated user data without waiting for the 30-day recovery period.

This may include:

* Personal tasks;  
* Routines;  
* Reminders;  
* Cloud profile information;  
* Notification-related information;  
* Local SQLite information; and  
* Other account-associated information covered by the deletion process.

The permanent wipe is intended to be irreversible.

Users should use this option only when they are certain they no longer want the associated account information and routines.
