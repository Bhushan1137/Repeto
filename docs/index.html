Privacy Policy

Application: Repeto (com.repeto.app) | Effective Date: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Repeto: Recurring Tasks & Chores ("Repeto", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our services on Android.

By installing or using Repeto, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum amount of data required to provide our recurring habit and chore management services:

  • Account Credentials: When signing in via Google Sign-In, we receive your email address, full name, and display avatar provided by Google's OAuth service to create and secure your user profile.
  • Task & Household Data: Tasks, due dates, recurrence frequencies, completion history, streaks, and household member IDs you create in the app. This data is synchronized to our secure Cloud Firestore database so you can access your tasks across devices and collaborate with household members.
  • Optional Profile Data: Phone number (optional, only if entered manually in your profile for member identification).

3. Permissions & On-Device Processing

Repeto requests specific permissions for core functionality:

Camera Permission (CAMERA): Repeto accesses the camera strictly and solely to scan QR codes when joining or inviting family and roommates to a shared Household. Camera frames are processed entirely on your device in real-time. Repeto never captures, records, saves, or uploads photos or videos from your camera.

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used solely to trigger timely push notifications for recurring tasks and habit due dates at the exact minute you schedule them, even when your device is idle.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display task reminders, daily summaries, and household chore nudges on your device.
  • Device Reboot Notification (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used solely to reschedule your pending local reminder alarms when your phone restarts.

4. How We Use & Share Your Data

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide core task scheduling, recurrence calculations, and household chore synchronization.
  • Deliver local reminders and push notifications.
  • Enable household members you explicitly invite to view and complete shared chores.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Security & Data Storage

All data transmitted between the Repeto application and our backend servers (Google Firebase Cloud Services) is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Access to task data is protected by strict database security rules ensuring that only authenticated users and confirmed household members can access their respective tasks.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your personal data:

In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal task history instantly inside the app by going to Profile → Delete Account.

Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request permanent deletion of your account and all stored data by emailing us at devstudio.hm@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the Google account used to register. Your account and data will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Repeto is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete such data.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
