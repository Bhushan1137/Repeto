Account & Data Deletion

Official deletion policy and request portal for Repeto (com.repeto.tasks)

Your Data, Your Control

At Repeto, we respect your right to own and delete your data at any time. When you request account deletion, we completely remove your personal profile, task history, and synchronization links from our servers.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All pending tasks, recurrence patterns, completed chore streaks, and household connections associated with your personal profile will be erased.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Repeto mobile app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the Repeto app.
  2. Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Actions section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and confirm by tapping Yes, Delete My Account.

Your local cache is immediately cleared, and your cloud profile is flagged for permanent purge.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app, lost access to your device, or prefer to request deletion via the web, you can submit a deletion request to our support team:

How to submit via email:

  • Send an email to: devstudio.hm@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Repeto
  • From address: Send the email from the Google email address registered with your Repeto account so we can verify ownership.
  • Body: State that you request full deletion of your Repeto account, tasks, and data.
Submit Deletion Request via Email

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Immediately: Your user profile, display name, email, registered phone number, avatar URL, individual tasks, recurrence schedules, reminders, and completed streak records.
  • Shared Household Chores: If you created chores inside a shared household, the shared chore history stays with the remaining household members without your personal identity.
  • Retention Timeline: All data is permanently purged from Cloud Firestore backups within 7 business days of verification. No data is retained for marketing or advertising.
